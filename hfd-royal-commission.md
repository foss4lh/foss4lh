# Royal Commission Survey (hfd-royal-commission)

## Metadata
- **Collection Start:** 1930
- **Publication Year:** 1939
- **Coverage:** Herefordshire (All Parishes)
- **Status:** Comprehensive Scans

## Description
The mid-1930s Royal Commission survey of nearly every parish in Herefordshire. 

### Key Highlights
- **Universal Coverage**: David ordered up the survey for almost every single parish.
- **High Visual Interest**: These are noted as being some of the most popular items when David gives public presentations on landscape history.
- **Spatio-temporal Snapshot**: Provides a high-resolution view of the county just before the changes of the late 20th century.
