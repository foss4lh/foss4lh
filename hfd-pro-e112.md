# Exchequer Records (E112) (hfd-pro-e112)

## Metadata
- **Collection Start:** 1558
- **Publication Year:** 1702
- **Coverage:** Herefordshire
- **Status:** Transcription in Progress

## Description
Scans and transcriptions of Exchequer (E112) bills and answers from the Public Record Office (National Archives). These documents detail legal disputes and land grants in the mediaeval and early modern periods.

### Key Highlights
- **Indenture Curiographs**: Fascinating mediaeval legal deeds with scalloped/zigzag edges. Many are unique and not held in the National Archives.
- **AI Transcription Proof**: Gemini (LLM) has successfully demonstrated the ability to read and summarize 14th-century Gothic script from these scans, a task David originally thought required a human paleographer.
- **Geographic Links**: The records mention specific locations like Bromfield, Salop, and Hereford fields.
