# David Lovelace Archive (foss4lh)

This project is dedicated to archiving, digitizing, and sharing over 30 years of landscape history and nature conservation data for Herefordshire, collected by David Lovelace.

## Project Vision
To create a modern, accessible, and geo-referenced archive of Herefordshire's history, from mediaeval legal deeds to 20th-century aerial photography and long-term ecological surveys.

## Repository Structure

### Data Repositories
- **[hfd-public-records-office](./hfd-public-records-office)**: Exchequer (E112) and PRO documents, including Latin transcription and indexing.
- **[hfd-tithe-surveys](./hfd-tithe-surveys)**: Tithe maps (1840s), field schedules, and georectified rasters.
- **[hfd-aerial-photography](./hfd-aerial-photography)**: Aerofilms archive (1920s-1980s) and modern high-res aerials.
- **[hfd-nature-surveys](./hfd-nature-surveys)**: 40 years of hedgerow surveys, habitat maps, and grassland data.

### Tooling & Presentation
- **[hfd-landscape-explorer](./hfd-landscape-explorer)**: The central web application for visualizing and exploring the archive.
- **[hfd-data-ops](./hfd-data-ops)**: Scripts for georectification, image compression, and data chunking.

### Legacy / Existing Repos
- **rotherwas-tiles**: Mapping project for the Rotherwas area.
- **foss4lh.github.io**: Project homepage and documentation.
- **foss4lh**: Core project materials and bookdown documentation.
