# Aerofilms Archive (hfd-aerofilms)

## Metadata
- **Collection Start:** 1920
- **Publication Year:** 1986
- **Coverage:** Herefordshire
- **Status:** Partially Indexed

## Description
A comprehensive collection of historic aerial photography covering Herefordshire. Includes high-resolution scans from the Aerofilms company, capturing the evolution of towns and rural areas over six decades.

## Access
High-resolution Sony Raw (`.arw`) files and optimized JPEGs.
