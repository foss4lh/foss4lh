# River Wye & NRA Archive (hfd-river-wye)

## Metadata
- **Collection Start:** 1950
- **Publication Year:** 2010
- **Coverage:** River Wye Corridor
- **Status:** Scanned / Mixed Formats

## Description
A collection of maps and documents from the Natural Rivers Authority (NRA) and other sources relating to the River Wye.

### Key Highlights
- **Bridge Building**: Technical and historical maps showing bridge construction and modifications.
- **1950 Flood**: Specific records of the 1950 flood events.
- **Ecological Management**: Data on the historical management of the river ecosystem.
