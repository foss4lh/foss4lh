# Multi-decadal Hedgerow Surveys (hfd-hedgerow-surveys)

## Metadata
- **Collection Start:** 1990
- **Publication Year:** 2025
- **Coverage:** Herefordshire
- **Status:** Ongoing / Digital

## Description
Long-term monitoring data of Herefordshire's hedgerows. This dataset includes species composition, condition assessments, and spatial data collected over 30+ years by David Lovelace.

### Key Components
- **The Birches (Nature Reserve)**: Extensive records for this specific transformation from agricultural land to wildlife haven, including rare plant data.
- **1948 Woodland Survey**: Post-war government survey of all woods in England. Includes species, age class, and timber state—frequently used by David for historical woodland analysis.
- **Grassland Records**: Valuable datasets on ancient grasslands.

## Access
Available as spreadsheets and GIS vector files.
